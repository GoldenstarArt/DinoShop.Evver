## Grind & Groove (homework planner)
`planner/index.html` is an hourly homework planner you can talk to. Type things like "busy saturday 14-16", "english essay due friday 2h", "done" or "I'm bored". It plans work into your free afternoons and puts rewards in between. Everything is saved in your browser.
